About Me

I'm Florian, a Dutch maker and systems builder currently pursuing a Bachelor's in Business Administration at the University of Groningen.

I thrive at the intersection of digital and physical making. Whether it's designing and 3D printing custom sim racing pedals with Arduino-based load cells, managing self-hosted server infrastructure, or building things with wood and metal — I'm driven by the satisfaction of turning concepts into working reality.

With hands-on experience in 3D printing, CAD design, electronics, system administration, and business operations, I bring a practical mix of technical skill, ownership, and problem-solving to every project.

I am Dutch and speak Dutch, German, and English, shaped by studying in the Netherlands and having lived in Germany.
